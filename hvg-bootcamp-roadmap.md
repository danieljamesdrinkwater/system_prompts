# HGV Bootcamp Roadmap

**Route:** Skills Bootcamp → Cat C+E → ADR → Tanker Work

**Starting position:** Clean licence, willingness to tramp.

---

## Phase 1 — Secure a Bootcamp Place (NOW)

Immediate actions, this week:

1. **Call South Essex College directly** — they've run Cat C+E (Class 1) bootcamps covering Thurrock and Basildon. Ask specifically whether they have a cohort opening from April 2026 onwards. Previous programmes have been 12–15 weeks and fully funded for unemployed adults aged 19+.

2. **Search the National Careers Service course finder** — go to [nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/find-a-course](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/find-a-course) and search "HGV" or "LGV" filtered to Skills Bootcamp. This is the central listings page for all live bootcamps nationally.

3. **Contact Essex County Council skills team** — Essex County Council's Wave 7 Skills Bootcamps procurement was expected around February 2026, which means new programmes could be launching imminently for the April 2026 financial year. Ask what HGV provision is being commissioned.

4. **Talk to your Work Coach** — tell them you want to do an HGV Skills Bootcamp as a work preparation activity. Frame it as a direct route to employment. Ask them to check the Manage Jobs and Opportunities system for bootcamp referrals in your area. Being on LCW means your Work Coach has discretion to support this — make the case that you're job-ready and this leads directly to a guaranteed interview.

5. **Register interest with HGVC** — [hgvc.co.uk/bootcamp](https://hgvc.co.uk/bootcamp). They deliver at 60 locations nationally and have had £10m in DfE funding. They've offered fully funded courses for individuals being recruited by employers.

---

## Phase 2 — Complete the Bootcamp (12–16 weeks)

The bootcamp covers everything at zero cost:
- Medical
- Provisional HGV licence application
- Theory prep
- Practical training
- CPC tests
- One re-sit per test

You come out the other end with a Cat C or C+E licence and a **guaranteed job interview**.

**Critical:** Make sure you get on a bootcamp that delivers **Cat C+E (Class 1)**, not just Cat C (Class 2). South Essex College's programme has done C+E previously. If the only available bootcamp near you is Cat C only, take it — but know you'll need to upgrade later, and that upgrade won't be free.

---

## Phase 3 — ADR Certification (1–2 weeks, ~£300–500)

This is the one cost you'll almost certainly need to self-fund. ADR courses run over 3–5 days depending on the modules.

**Modules needed for tanker work:**
- Core module
- Class 3 (flammable liquids)
- Tanks specialisation

Some employers will put you through ADR after hiring you, but having it upfront makes you significantly more attractive and opens the door to the £50k+ roles immediately.

---

## Phase 4 — Get Hired into Tanker Work

With C+E + ADR + willingness to tramp, target roles like the one in Grays paying £58,760.

**Target area:** Essex/Thames Gateway corridor — Purfleet, Grays, Thurrock, Tilbury. This is a hotspot for fuel and chemical distribution.

**Realistic salary progression:**
- **Starting:** £38–45k (newly qualified)
- **12–18 months in:** £50–60k with experience

## Tramping Tech Kit — Peli Case

Everything suction-mount or magnetic so you leave no trace in someone else's cab. Fits a Peli 1510 or similar.

### Power
- 12V multi-socket splitter (compact)
- 12V to 240V inverter (pocket size, ~150W)
- USB-C PD charger + cables
- Power bank (20,000mAh+)

### Navigation & Comms
- Truck sat nav (Garmin Dezl)
- Magnetic dash mount
- Phone + spare phone
- DAB radio (pocket, battery powered)

### Internet
- Starlink Mini (flat panel, ~30x25cm — sits flat in case lid)
- Starlink 12V power adapter (or run via inverter)
- Starlink magnetic roof mount (no drill)
- ~£225 hardware + ~£40/month roam plan

### Entertainment & Downtime
- Tablet or laptop
- Bluetooth earbuds + over-ears (noise cancelling for sleeping)
- USB stick / portable SSD with media

### Dash & Security
- Dash cam (suction mount, no hardwire)
- Magnetic phone mount

### Lighting
- USB rechargeable headtorch
- Small LED lantern (magnetic base — stick to cab roof)

### Connectivity (Starlink backup)
- Mobile Wi-Fi hotspot
- SIM card (unlimited data)

### Hacking & Radio
- Flipper Zero (sub-GHz, RFID, NFC, infrared, GPIO)
- Useful for testing depot access fobs, tyre pressure sensors, truck remote diagnostics

### Misc
- Multi-tool
- Cable ties + velcro straps (tidy cables, remove when leaving cab)
- Microfibre cloth (screen cleaning)

## Retrofit AI Driving Assist Hardware

Aftermarket ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems) that can be fitted to existing HGVs without permanent modification:

### Bitsensing ADAS Kit
- Radar + camera retrofit package for trucks and buses
- Blind Spot Information System, Forward Collision Warning, Rear Collision Warning, Surround View Monitoring
- Meets UNECE R151 (blind spot) and R159 (moving off) regulations
- Pilot results: 51% improvement in driver safety scores, 24% reduction in pedestrian collision warnings
- [bitsensing.com](https://bitsensing.com)

### Mobileye Shield+
- Camera-based collision avoidance, retrofit for HGVs
- Pedestrian and cyclist detection, lane departure, forward collision warnings
- Visual and audio alerts via driver display
- Widely used by UK fleets (TfL mandated for London operations)
- [mobileye.com](https://www.mobileye.com)

### WABCO (ZF) OnSide / OnGuardACTIVE
- Blind spot detection retrofit kits
- OnGuardACTIVE adds radar-based autonomous emergency braking
- Bolt-on kits for most popular truck platforms
- [zf.com](https://www.zf.com)

### Gauzy Safety Tech
- Camera monitor systems (CMS) + ADAS, fully homologated
- Retrofit or OE fit, eliminates blind spots with real-time alerts
- [gauzy.com](https://www.gauzy.com/safety-tech-adas/)

### SerVision ADAS
- Video processing collision avoidance for heavy trucks
- Approved in EU, Israel, South Korea, Japan, China
- [servision.net](https://servision.net/adas-hgv/)

### Coming Soon — Full Autonomy
- **Wayve AI Driver** — hardware-agnostic L2+ to L4, optimised for goods delivery
- **Pony AI Gen-4** — autonomous truck kit, mass production expected 2026, 70% cost reduction vs previous gen

> **Note:** Most retrofit ADAS is fleet-level kit — talk to your employer about fitting. The Mobileye and Bitsensing units are the most practical for individual drivers to advocate for.
