# HGV Bootcamp Roadmap

**Route:** Skills Bootcamp → Cat C+E → ADR → Tanker Work

**Starting position:** Clean licence, willingness to tramp.

---

## Phase 1 — Secure a Bootcamp Place (NOW)

Immediate actions, this week:

1. **Call South Essex College directly** — they've run Cat C+E (Class 1) bootcamps covering Thurrock and Basildon. Ask specifically whether they have a cohort opening from April 2026 onwards. Previous programmes have been 12–15 weeks and fully funded for unemployed adults aged 19+.

2. **Search the National Careers Service course finder** — go to [nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/find-a-course](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/find-a-course) and search "HGV" or "LGV" filtered to Skills Bootcamp. This is the central listings page for all live bootcamps nationally.

3. **Contact Essex County Council skills team** — Essex County Council's Wave 7 Skills Bootcamps procurement was expected around February 2026, which means new programmes could be launching imminently for the April 2026 financial year. Ask what HGV provision is being commissioned.

4. **Talk to your Work Coach** — tell them you want to do an HGV Skills Bootcamp as a work preparation activity. Frame it as a direct route to employment. Ask them to check the Manage Jobs and Opportunities system for bootcamp referrals in your area. Being on LCW means your Work Coach has discretion to support this — make the case that you're job-ready and this leads directly to a guaranteed interview.

5. **Register interest with HGVC** — [hgvc.co.uk/bootcamp](https://hgvc.co.uk/bootcamp). They deliver at 60 locations nationally and have had £10m in DfE funding. They've offered fully funded courses for individuals being recruited by employers.

---

## Phase 2 — Complete the Bootcamp (12–16 weeks)

The bootcamp covers everything at zero cost:
- Medical
- Provisional HGV licence application
- Theory prep
- Practical training
- CPC tests
- One re-sit per test

You come out the other end with a Cat C or C+E licence and a **guaranteed job interview**.

**Critical:** Make sure you get on a bootcamp that delivers **Cat C+E (Class 1)**, not just Cat C (Class 2). South Essex College's programme has done C+E previously. If the only available bootcamp near you is Cat C only, take it — but know you'll need to upgrade later, and that upgrade won't be free.

---

## Phase 3 — ADR Certification (1–2 weeks, ~£300–500)

This is the one cost you'll almost certainly need to self-fund. ADR courses run over 3–5 days depending on the modules.

**Modules needed for tanker work:**
- Core module
- Class 3 (flammable liquids)
- Tanks specialisation

Some employers will put you through ADR after hiring you, but having it upfront makes you significantly more attractive and opens the door to the £50k+ roles immediately.

---

## Phase 4 — Get Hired into Tanker Work

With C+E + ADR + willingness to tramp, target roles like the one in Grays paying £58,760.

**Target area:** Essex/Thames Gateway corridor — Purfleet, Grays, Thurrock, Tilbury. This is a hotspot for fuel and chemical distribution.

**Realistic salary progression:**
- **Starting:** £38–45k (newly qualified)
- **12–18 months in:** £50–60k with experience
